# UBDS Validation — Data Sources

All external data used to validate the Universal Brine Dispersion Solver (UBDS),
with the kernel each source validates.

## Near-field jet/plume kernel
1. **Fischer, H.B., List, E.J., Koh, R.C.Y., Imberger, J. & Brooks, N.H. (1979).**
   *Mixing in Inland and Coastal Waters.* Academic Press, New York.
   — Canonical round-jet self-similar dilution law (bulk dilution ≈ 0.25–0.32·z/D)
   and jet spreading rate (db/dz ≈ 0.10–0.11). Validates the momentum-jet limit.
2. **Morton, B.R., Taylor, G.I. & Turner, J.S. (1956).** Turbulent gravitational
   convection from maintained and instantaneous sources. *Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A*
   **234**, 1–23. — Pure-plume volume-flux 5/3 power law. Validates the buoyancy-
   driven (plume) limit and the entrainment closure.
3. **Papakonstantis, I.G., Christodoulou, G.C. & Papanicolaou, P.N. (2011).**
   Inclined negatively buoyant jets 1: geometrical characteristics; 2: concentration
   measurements. *J. Hydraulic Research* **49**(1), 3–22.
   — Dimensionless terminal rise z_t/(D·Fr) ≈ 1.6–2.2 and return-point dilution
   S_r/Fr ≈ 0.4–0.6 for 60° inclined dense jets. Validates the dense (brine) jet.
4. **Roberts, P.J.W., Ferrier, A. & Daviero, G. (1997).** Mixing in inclined dense
   jets. *J. Hydraulic Engineering* **123**(8), 693–699. — Independent corroboration
   of inclined dense-jet dilution.
5. **Lee, J.H.W. & Chu, V.H. (2003).** *Turbulent Jets and Plumes — A Lagrangian
   Approach.* Kluwer. — Combined shear + forced (cross-flow) entrainment hypothesis.
6. **Jirka, G.H. (2004).** Integral model for turbulent buoyant jets in unbounded
   stratified flows, Part 1. *Environmental Fluid Mechanics* **4**, 1–56.
   — Reference integral-model formulation.

## Equation of state
7. **Millero, F.J. & Poisson, A. (1981).** International one-atmosphere equation of
   state of seawater. *Deep-Sea Research* **28A**, 625–629; **UNESCO (1981)**, Tenth
   report of the joint panel on oceanographic tables and standards, *UNESCO Tech.
   Papers Mar. Sci.* **36**. — EOS-80 reference densities (S ≤ 42 psu).
8. **CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics.** — Saturated NaCl brine density
   (~1200 kg/m³) anchoring the hypersaline extension.

## Hydrodynamics
9. Synthetic ADCP current-meter and tidal-level dataset generated by the author
   for this case study (bottom-mounted upward-looking profilers; neap and spring
   windows). Used to demonstrate the calibration workflow and skill metrics
   (RMSE, Willmott index of agreement, Nash–Sutcliffe efficiency). The tidal
   constituents (M2, S2, K1, O1) follow a documented meso-tidal semidiurnal regime.

## Site-specific case data
10. All site-specific inputs — bathymetry, tidal constituents, ambient
    salinity/temperature, brine chemistry and trace-impurity concentrations,
    diffuser geometry — are **synthetic but engineering-credible** values defined by
    the author (see `case_inputs.py`). None are drawn from any real consent.
    Environmental Quality Standards used for the impurity comparison follow typical
    marine-water EQS values.
